How the AI decides — every 2 seconds

Not a rigid algorithm — a reinforcement learning model trained on DSM traffic patterns, continuously improving.

1. Observe
All 400+ junctions report queue lengths, speeds, bus positions.
2. Predict
Model forecasts queue growth 3 minutes ahead on each road segment.
3. Optimise
Calculates green durations to minimise total city-wide waiting time.
4. Override
Ambulance or fire beacon → all signals on that corridor clear instantly.
